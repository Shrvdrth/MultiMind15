The Problem

Making decisions is hard

Important decisions deserve more than a single perspective — but gathering experts takes time and money.

🎯

Confirmation bias — we unconsciously favour information that confirms what we already believe.

🗣️

Groupthink — teams converge too fast, suppressing dissenting voices and missing critical risks.

⏰

Time pressure — you need analysis now, not after two weeks of meetings and workshops.

💡

MultiMind solves this — three AI agents with opposing mandates debate your question in seconds.

Solution

What is MultiMind?

1. You ask a question

Any business or technical decision — build vs. buy, architecture choices, hiring, pricing strategy.

2. Three AI agents analyse it

Strategist, Risk Analyst, and Engineer each respond independently — no groupthink.

3. They debate each other

In Round 2, agents read each other's responses and challenge, defend, or refine their positions.

4. A Moderator synthesises

Final recommendation with a 0–100 confidence score, consensus points, and key disagreements.
